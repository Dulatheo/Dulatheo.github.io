# Document Scanner Support

Need help or found a bug? Email **[d.yessilbayev@gmail.com](mailto:d.yessilbayev@gmail.com)** and include your device model, OS version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days.

## FAQ

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
- **iPhone / iPad:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Document Scanner → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap the **PRO** badge in the app and choose **Manage Subscription**.
- **Android:** Google Play Store → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Document Scanner → Cancel.

To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends.

**I paid but Premium isn't unlocked. What should I do?**
Open the paywall and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

**I got a new phone. How do I get Premium back?**
Install the app, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then tap **Restore Purchases**.

**Where are my documents stored? Can I move them to a new device?**
Documents are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud. To keep a document, export it (for example as a PDF) and save it to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email before you switch devices.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases go through Apple or Google, so refund requests are handled by them:
- Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- Google Play: [Google Play refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)
