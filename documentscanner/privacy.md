# Privacy Policy

**Document Scanner** · Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Document Scanner app ("the app", "we") for iOS and Android handles your information. In short: **your documents stay on your device. We don't collect, store or sell your personal data.**

## Data the app processes

- **Scans and documents.** Pages you scan or import, the documents you create, signatures, comments and exported files are stored **only on your device**. They are never uploaded to our servers. We don't operate any servers for the app.
- **Text recognition (OCR).** Text recognition runs **on your device**. Your images are not sent anywhere for processing.
- **Camera.** The app uses the camera only to scan pages when you start a scan.
- **Photos (iOS).** The app accesses your photo library only when you choose to import a photo as a page or save an export to Photos.
- **Sharing and export.** When you share or export a document, it goes only to the app or destination you pick (for example Mail, Files or a messenger). Their own privacy policies then apply.

## Data we don't collect

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers.

## Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed through the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store them. The app only receives confirmation of whether your subscription is active. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Third-party components

On Android, document scanning and text recognition use Google ML Kit, which runs on your device through Google Play services. Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about these components under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data retention and deletion

Because all your data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete individual documents in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
